About

I study human memory and cognition, combining behavioral, neuroimaging, and statistical methods to understand how the brain supports memory across cultures and aging. Currently, I work with Dr. Audrey Duarte at the Memory and Aging Lab, where I study cultural differences in how the brain processes object-context relationships in episodic memory. During my undergrad, I worked with Dr. Thad Polk at the Computational & Cognitive Neuroscience Lab and Dr. Shinobu Kitayama at the Culture & Cognition Lab. I'm also interested in applying data analysis and research skills to real-world, industry problems.
